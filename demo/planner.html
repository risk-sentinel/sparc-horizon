How the planner computes dates

Each phase has a base effort in engineer-weeks and a maximum number of people who can usefully work on it at once. When several phases become ready at the same moment, free engineers are split between them, and each extra person on a phase adds 10% coordination overhead. AI-assisted coding reduces effort by 20%. The buffer is applied to every phase, not tacked on at the end, so slips show up where they would really happen.

Bars outlined in dark ink are on the critical path: moving any of them moves the pilot date. The MVP marker is the end of the attestation workflow, which is the first point where an ISO can close a manual control end to end. The Pilot marker is the end of hardening.
